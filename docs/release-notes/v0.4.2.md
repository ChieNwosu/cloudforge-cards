# CloudForge Cards v0.4.2: Track-Aware AWS Learning Platform

**Live demo:** [https://cloudforge-cards.emergent.host/](https://cloudforge-cards.emergent.host/)

**Submitted to:** Emergent Builder's Contest (2026-06)

---

## Summary

v0.4.2 adds track-aware Play mode with certification track filtering, expands the service card pool to 60 cards and the scenario pool to 38, and adds DEA (Data Engineer Associate) track support across all learning and gameplay modes. This release was submitted to the Emergent Builder's Contest.

---

## Key Features

### Track-Aware Play

- New track selector on the Play start screen: CLF/SAA, AIF, MLA, DEA, and Mixed
- Each track filters scenarios and service-card hands to certification-relevant content
- Track-aware service-card pools provide relevant cards, fair distractors, and guaranteed ideal-combo availability
- Mixed mode combines all tracks for a comprehensive review

### Content Expansion

- 60 service cards (expanded from 33) across 11 categories
- 38 Play scenarios (expanded from 10) with domain-specific architecture challenges
- AIF, MLA, and DEA flashcard expansion with original concept cards
- AIF, MLA, and DEA Test Mode question expansion with server-side grading
- AIF, MLA, and DEA Match Mode prompt expansion with partial credit and review feedback

### DEA Track Support

- DEA (AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate) added across Learn Hub, Flashcards, Test Mode, Match Mode, and Play Mode
- Data lake, ETL, streaming, and analytics services tagged for DEA relevance
- DEA-specific Play scenarios covering data pipeline architecture challenges

### Deck Isolation

- Learn Flashcards deck remains isolated from the Play expansion cards (study deck stability)
- Play card pool expanded independently
- This ensures the flashcard study experience is not disrupted by new game-only cards

## Known Limitations

- Landing page hero stats may show stale numbers (30 cards, 10 scenarios) until frontend static text is updated
- Local progress, XP, and scores do not sync across devices (localStorage only)
- No authentication or user accounts
- Professor Flock hints are static text, not AI-generated
- Voice availability depends on browser and OS
